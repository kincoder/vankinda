About

Technology is the medium, not the goal. That's been the constant across two decades of designing and building business-critical software, from stakeholder conversation through to production, most recently as a tech lead in enterprise finance.

Vankinda is where that same care gets applied to smaller businesses: the ones who need a system connected, a platform migrated, or a manual process turned into something that runs itself, without hiring a whole department to do it.

No account managers, no hand-offs. You work directly with the person who does the work.
